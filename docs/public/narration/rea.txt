Reverse Engineer Anything, or R E A, connects an AI agent to software inspection tools. Official game examples investigate sound panning in DX-Ball and bullet-angle calculations in Touhou Four. A community project reports reconstructing a Ski or Die minigame in Rust with S D L Two. We have reviewed those reports, not independently reproduced them. Our local test found logs and saved data rather than a complete game executable. Next, we should investigate one small mechanic with known source code and check the recovered behavior against ground truth.
