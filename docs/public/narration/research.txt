Our game research has four tracks: programming, audio, three-dimensional assets, and integration. We want to learn what advanced AI can do reliably, and where human work remains. We will name the exact tools and versions, preserve failed attempts, and compare results against clear acceptance tests. Our first REA trial inventoried local files; it did not recover gameplay code. The next step is to choose one small task and gather evidence, rather than select a complete technology stack from demonstrations alone.
