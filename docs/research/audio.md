# Audio & voice

<AudioSummary id="audio" />

Can AI-generated audio communicate gameplay events while maintaining a consistent sonic identity?

::: info Research status
Local Kokoro narration has been tested for research summaries. Game sound effects, ambience, and music remain untested; no game-audio tool has been selected.
:::

## First experiment: EXP-002 — One room, one soundscape

1. Define a sound brief for ambience and three interactive effects.
2. Generate alternatives, retain prompts and source terms, and select a coherent set.
3. Integrate the sounds into a room and test triggering, loop seams, and levels.

## What to measure

- Fit to the brief and consistency
- Loop quality, noise, clipping, and intelligibility
- Editing time and export compatibility
- Provenance and documented usage rights

## Next step

Choose candidates, record dated official sources, and create a test using the [experiment template](../experiments/template.md). Publish the evidence in the [register](../experiments/index.md).
