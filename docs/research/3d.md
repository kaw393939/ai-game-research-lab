## First experiment: EXP-003 — One engine-ready prop

1. Specify one prop, its dimensions, style, and triangle and texture budgets.
2. Generate the mesh and materials; save all intermediate outputs.
3. Inspect topology, UVs, scale, collisions, and import behavior in the chosen engine.

## What to measure

- Silhouette and adherence to the reference
- Topology, UVs, materials, and texture artifacts
- Cleanup time and asset size
- Runtime performance and rig suitability when applicable
