Our research should move from evidence to one playable room. Start by separating documented interfaces, author-reported demonstrations, independently reproduced results, and features validated in our game. First compare a small editable prop, a precision part, and a simple gameplay mechanic. Give candidates the same brief and a clear time budget. Ask for a revision after the first success. Measure total human effort, including cleanup and integration. Only expand to complex characters or large environments after the small assets and mechanics pass their tests. These are proposed experiments, not completed results.
