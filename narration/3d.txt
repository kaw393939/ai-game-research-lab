A good render does not prove that a model is ready for a game. Our proposed first three-dimensional asset experiment follows one prop from its brief into the engine. We will define its dimensions, style, triangle budget, and texture budget before generation. Then we will inspect topology, texture coordinates, scale, materials, and collision. We will also record cleanup time and runtime performance. The key question is whether the asset can be imported, revised, and used without disproportionate human repair. This experiment has not yet been run.
