The integration track brings code, sound, and art into one small playable room. First, we will choose a target platform and engine from the game requirements. Then we will combine one gameplay loop, a generated prop, and a soundscape. We will test the packaged build on the target hardware and measure frame time, loading, memory, and cross-system defects. Individually impressive outputs are only useful if they work together. This vertical slice is proposed; we have not yet built or benchmarked it.
