Our audio research asks whether generated sound can communicate gameplay events and maintain a consistent style. We have now tested local Kokoro speech generation for these research summaries. That is a narration test, not a validation of game music or sound effects. Our proposed game experiment is one room with ambience and three interactive effects. We will check triggering, loop seams, levels, creative fit, editing time, and usage rights. The goal is a coherent soundscape that works during play.
